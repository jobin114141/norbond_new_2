OUR BEGINNING

At Norbond, we believe in creating legacies, not just businesses. Rooted in a rich tradition of craftsmanship, innovation, and a commitment to excellence, we empower a portfolio of brands that transcend borders, redefine industries, and embody the very essence of luxury, quality, and sustainability.

Founded with a vision to unite distinct brands under one powerful umbrella, Norbond is more than a corporate entity — it's an embodiment of passion, creativity, and resilience. We operate with a global presence, from our Middle East headquarters in Dubai to regional offices in India, the UK, and Saudi Arabia.

WHAT DRIVES US

Mission & Vision

01

Mission

Our mission at Norbond is simple yet powerful: To be the home of exquisite brands that elevate everyday experiences, offering products that combine innovation, craftsmanship, and sustainability.

  • Cultivate creativity and craftsmanship in every product we create.
  • Lead with sustainability at the forefront of our business practices.
  • Elevate global lifestyles by blending luxury with purpose.
  • Expand globally while maintaining deep local connections.
02

Vision

We aspire to redefine luxury — to create a future where every brand under our umbrella is a symbol of excellence, where innovation meets timeless quality and cultural heritage.

We envision a world where our brands contribute to shaping a lifestyle that values beauty, comfort, sustainability, and ethical responsibility — forging partnerships that reflect integrity, trust, and passion.

OUR JOURNEY

The Journey
So Far

From its inception, Norbond has always focused on one key thing: Quality. Over the years, we have expanded across multiple industries — each venture showcasing a dedication to meticulous craftsmanship and an unwavering pursuit of excellence.

The story began with a passion for fine craftsmanship and grew into a global network of brands, each offering products that evoke beauty and comfort. Today, Norbond operates across the UAE, India, the UK, and KSA, with plans for continued expansion into new markets.

Our brands — Luxe Decora, La Chariot, Fatbag, Jumbble, and Bean Bagss — have steadily established themselves as leaders in their respective industries, embodying our core values of premium experience, design, and sustainability.
